Selection

Click a fire on the map to open its popup and toggle its selection. Selected fires are highlighted on the map.

Delete Selected only hides features from the map view. The ArcGIS FeatureServer is never modified.

Add a new fire point

The new point is saved to your own backend (saved_fires.json) — it is not written back to the ArcGIS FeatureServer. It is drawn as an orange diamond.
